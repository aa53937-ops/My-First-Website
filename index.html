How to put together a PB&J Sandwich

Ingredients

  • Peanut butter
  • Jelly
  • Two slices of bread

Instructions

  1. Spread one piece of bread with peanut butter.
  2. Spread the other piece of bread with jelly.
  3. Sandwich the bread together with the spread sides inward.
  4. Place sandwich on a plate.
  5. Cut sandwich in half.
  6. Serve immediately.
